# TikTok

Publish-only for us. **TikTok exposes no comment API at all** — not a limitation
to work around, not something to retry. Never tell the founder you're watching
TikTok comments.

## What decides reach

Watch time and completion, then rewatches. Follower count barely matters — every
post is auditioned to strangers, which is why this is the channel where a small
account can still get seen.

The first two seconds decide everything. Not "the hook is important" — the video
is either watched or scrolled in that window, and nothing later recovers it.

## The caption

**The first line is a hook, never a description.** It sits under the video
competing with the video for attention, so it either adds something or it's
noise. Describing what the viewer can already see is the most common waste.

Short. Two lines is generous. Anything longer gets truncated behind "more" and
the tail is read by nobody.

## Hashtags

Moderate weight — discovery leans on sound and topic signals more than tags. **A
few specific ones beat many broad ones.** Three to five.

`#fyp` and its variants do nothing. They are a tell that the account is trying
rather than a lever that works.

Selected from mined sets, never invented.

## Sound

Sounds are pure distribution. **The bridge test applies to the idea, never to
the audio** — a trending sound doesn't need to relate to the product, it needs
to carry the post into a feed.

Catch a sound rising, not at peak. By the time a sound is obviously everywhere
the advantage is gone and using it reads as late.

## Photo mode

A carousel of stills is a first-class format here, not a fallback. It costs
nothing to make from real screenshots and it competes on the same surface as
video. Swipe-through is the completion signal.

## Register

Handheld, badly lit, jump-cut mid-word, starts before the speaker is ready.
**Too produced is the tell.** A clean corporate edit reads as an ad and gets
treated as one.

## Consent — TikTok's requirement, not our caution

**The founder must confirm the rendered preview before anything posts.** That is
TikTok's own legal requirement for API-published content, and it is stated to
the founder as theirs rather than as our carefulness. A post that goes out
without a confirmed preview is a compliance problem, not a UX preference.

## What matters

Completion rate and shares.
