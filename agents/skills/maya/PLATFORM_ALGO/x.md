# X

Text and conversation. **This is the channel where replies matter more than
posts** — the reply surface is where a small account is actually visible, and
the timeline is where it isn't.

## What decides reach

Replies and quotes, then dwell. The system rewards conversation, so a post that
starts an argument outperforms a post that gets agreement.

Links are downranked. Put the link in a reply to your own post, not in the post.

## Length

**280 weighted characters.** A URL counts as exactly 23 no matter its real
length; CJK and emoji weigh 2. `preflight` computes this — never count
characters by hand.

Threads work when the first post stands alone. A first post that only makes
sense as a setup for the second is a first post nobody expands.

## Hashtags

**One or two maximum.** More reads as spam and is a reliable marker of an
automated account. Often zero is correct — hashtags do very little here and
their main effect is on how the account looks.

Selected from mined sets, never invented.

## Register

Lowercase is normal. Fragments are normal. Ending without a conclusion is
normal. The register that works here is closest to how the founder already
writes, which makes it the channel where their own voice needs least
translation.

The tell to avoid: a post that reads like a press release trimmed to fit.

## What matters

Replies and profile clicks, not impressions. Threads earn attention, they don't
assume it.
