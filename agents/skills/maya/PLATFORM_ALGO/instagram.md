# Instagram

Reels are the surface that reaches non-followers. Feed posts and stories serve
the people who already follow.

## What decides reach

Sends and saves outrank likes by a wide margin. A post someone forwards to one
friend is worth more than a hundred passive likes, because the share is the
distribution.

Watch time on Reels, same as TikTok. Completion and rewatch.

## The caption

**First line is a hook.** It is what shows before "more", and the truncation
point is roughly 125 characters — write as though the rest may never be read,
because usually it isn't.

Longer captions do work here in a way they don't on TikTok, but only when the
first line earned it.

## Hashtags

Still carry reach on Reels, **less than they once did** — topic and audio
signals now dominate. Three to five, specific, in the caption rather than a
comment. A wall of thirty is a spam signal now, not a growth hack.

Selected from mined sets, never invented.

## The same asset as TikTok

Reels take the same 9:16 vertical. **The asset is shared; the caption is not.**
An identical caption across both is one of the most recognisable automation
tells there is — the accounts that do it are visibly running a scheduler.

⚠️ A visible TikTok watermark is downranked. Publish the clean asset.

## Register

Slightly more composed than TikTok, still not corporate. The line is that
Instagram tolerates something that looks made; it does not tolerate something
that looks advertised.

## What matters

Saves and sends, not likes. The cover frame is doing more work than the caption.
