# Comment relay

Rules and channels are in `CONVENTIONS.md`.

**TikTok exposes no comment API to anyone.** That is a platform wall, not a
limitation of ours. So the choice is not automated-versus-relayed, it is
relayed-versus-nothing — and commenting on a climbing video is one of the
cheapest ways a founder reaches an audience that is already paying attention.

Done by hand, in their voice, from their account, this is also *better* than
automation would be. It carries weight an obviously-automated comment never
does, and it cannot get their account actioned.

## What to look for

From the daily sweep, the posts that are **climbing now** — engagement against
age, not raw size. A six-hour-old video pulling comments is worth more than
last week's winner, because the author and the audience are both still there.

Prefer where the founder can say something only they could say: someone hitting
the exact problem their product solves, asking a question they have answered a
hundred times, or wrong about something in a way they can correct kindly.

Skip anything where the honest comment is an advert.

## Two or three a day. Not ten

This costs them attention, and attention is what they have least of. Two
genuinely good ones beat ten they will not send.

## What to send

One message per opportunity, and make it a ten-second job:

> *"@handle's video on pricing SaaS solo is climbing — 40k views in five hours,
> comments full of people asking how to charge. Here's what I'd say:*
>
> *'the thing nobody tells you is your first price is a question, not a
> decision. we moved ours three times in year one and each time we learned more
> from who left than who stayed.'*
>
> *[link] — paste and go, or tell me to redraft."*

The link, the draft, and nothing else. No preamble, no explanation of why
commenting matters.

## Write it as a comment, not an advert

- **Never mention the product unless it is genuinely the answer**, and even
  then, once and plainly.
- Add something. A comment that only agrees is noise.
- Their voice, their register, their level of formality. You have their
  writing — use it.
- Short. Comments are read on a phone between other things.
- Never open with a compliment to buy the right to sell.

## Follow up once, then let it go

If they post it, note what it got — that is a real result and belongs in the
weekly report. If they do not, do not ask twice. Adjust what you send
tomorrow instead: the thing they skipped is data about what they will not say.
