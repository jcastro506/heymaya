# Pick the week

Rules and channels are in `CONVENTIONS.md`. This skill is about what to make,
and when to decide it.

**Decide once a week, not once a day.** Seven separate daily decisions produce
seven unrelated posts, and a founder cannot tell whether anything is working. A
week built on one shape can be judged.

## Monday: pick one shape

Read what the sweeps brought back and rank it by how much someone else has
already risked on it:

1. **A competitor ad still running after three weeks.** Someone pays every day
   to keep it alive and has a dashboard telling them to. Strongest signal there
   is.
2. **An organic post beating its own account's baseline.** Not the biggest post
   — the one outperforming what that account usually does.
3. **A complaint a real buyer typed.** Unmet demand, unproven format.
4. **A hashtag or trend.** Presence, not proof. Use only when nothing above
   exists.

Take the strongest thing you actually have. Say which rung it came from when
you propose it — "this ad has been running 24 days" is a reason; "this felt
good" is not.

## Then derive five to seven variations

Same shape, different angle each time. Vary the hook, the objection answered,
the screen you open on. **Do not vary everything** — if every post differs in
every way, nothing is learned.

Reposting the same asset does not count. Platforms dampen it, so the fourth
posting reaches fewer people than the first.

## Ask once

Send the shape, why you chose it, and the variations. One message, one
question, one yes.

Then execute. Tuesday through Sunday you are not deciding anything — you are
working through a list you already agreed. If a variation turns out to be
impossible, say so and move to the next one; do not start a fresh negotiation.

**A week with one ask is a week they can say yes to. Seven asks is a week they
stop reading.**

## Retire the week when it ends

When the week is done, the leftovers go with it. An idea that did not get made
this week is not automatically next week's — it competes again on evidence,
against everything new. Otherwise the bank fills with things that were never
good enough to build and they keep winning by seniority.

## What you owe them at the end

Which variation did best, what you think that means, and what changes next
week. One paragraph. If nothing worked, say that plainly and say what you are
changing — a week with no result is information too.
