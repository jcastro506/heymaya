# R4 — Competitive landscape for HeyMaya service product (2026-04-27)

> Scope: HeyMaya for **service businesses** (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, cleaning, roofing, garage-door, pest, restoration, mobile detailing, mobile grooming — the 1-truck to 25-tech operator). Not the creator product. Not the multi-location enterprise.

---

## TL;DR — The gap HeyMaya fills

Every existing tool in the service-business stack is either **a feature buried in a $250–$500/tech CRM** (ServiceTitan Marketing Pro, Housecall Pro AI Team, Jobber AI), **a single-channel point tool** (Birdeye for reviews, Podium for SMS, Hootsuite for social, Avoca for phones), **or a $300–$900/mo "communications platform"** that only does the inbound phone/SMS slice (Hatch, Avoca, Numa, Quo/OpenPhone+Sona). **Nothing on the market is a single proactive AI manager that lives in the operator's text thread, owns reviews + social + brand voice + lead follow-up + accountability across the whole business, and is priced for a 1–10 person shop ($99–$499) instead of an enterprise.** The wedge: **"Your AI marketing manager before you can afford a human marketing manager"** — proactive, multi-channel, grounded, and bundled at a flat price. ServiceTitan customers can't get this without Marketing Pro + Phones Pro + outside agency = $1.5k+/mo. Sub-ServiceTitan operators (the Jobber/Housecall/no-CRM majority) can't get it at all today without stitching 4–6 SaaS subscriptions.

**Two-axis read.**

## Detailed findings

### Tier 1 — Review / reputation

#### Birdeye
**Target:** 150,000+ businesses, sweet spot is multi-location SMB → enterprise. Won G2 #1 reputation management 10 straight times.
**Pricing:** $299 (Starter) / $349 (Growth) / $449 (Dominate) per location/mo, plus $5–15k onboarding for multi-location. 4+ locations forces a custom "Premium" quote.
**What they do well:** Strongest AI agent layer in the reputation space — Review Generation Agent picks the optimal channel/time to ask for a review; Review Response Agent writes replies in your tone; AI social posts. 150+ review-site coverage. Multi-location dashboard is best-in-class.
**What they do poorly:** Per-location pricing destroys single-truck operators ($299/mo for 1 review tool is steep for a guy doing $300k/yr). Annual contracts default. Onboarding fee is brutal. The AI is **reactive** — it responds to events; it doesn't proactively text the owner saying "Hey, your last 3 jobs in 78704 didn't get review requests, I sent them — also, you've slipped to 4.6 stars in that ZIP, here's why."
**AI features specifically:** Generative review responses with image/sentiment interpretation. AI listing optimizer. AI social caption generation. Conversational AI for surfacing trends.
**Missing:** No outbound social strategy. No brand voice that lives across channels. Not in the operator's text thread.
**Reputation:** Strong, but expensive and fundamentally an enterprise sales motion.
**Trajectory:** Doubling down on agentic AI ("Reputation Agents" branding), pushing further enterprise.
**Gap they leave:** The single-location operator who needs an agent that owns reviews **plus** the rest of marketing, at <$200/mo.

#### Podium
**Target:** 100,000+ local SMBs across home services, automotive, healthcare, retail. Single-location strength.
**Pricing:** Core $399 / Pro $599 / Signature custom. AI Employee "Jerry" (now GPT-5.1) is an add-on. Quote-based.
**What they do well:** Best-in-class text-first inbox. Strong web-chat-to-text capture. Payments built in. Fast time-to-value on lead capture. Jerry is genuinely good at lead capture/scheduling/follow-up across channels.
**What they do poorly:** No social media management at all. Quote-based pricing wastes operator time. Add-ons stack quickly — real bill is $600–$1,000/mo. Annual contract default. Heavy sales cycle.
**AI features specifically:** Jerry the AI Employee — handles inbound web/SMS leads, schedules, follows up, multi-channel context-aware.
**Missing:** Social posting/strategy. Reviews are good but bolt-on. No proactive marketing brain.
**Reputation:** Loved on the inbox UX, hated on the contract+price model.
**Trajectory:** Aggressively positioning Jerry as "AI Employee" tier — the wedge they're trying to build is "a $1k/mo AI worker is cheaper than a $4k/mo CSR." Reasonable thesis.
**Gap they leave:** They own inbound (lead-to-text-to-booked). They don't own the marketing/growth/reputation strategy layer that makes the lead show up in the first place.

#### NiceJob
**Target:** Solo and small home services — exactly HeyMaya's crowd.
**Pricing:** $75 (Reviews) / $125 (Pro). 14-day trial, no contract. **The friendliest pricing in the category.**
**What they do well:** Cheap. Good Jobber/HCP/FieldPulse integrations. AI review replies. Social-proof widgets. No-contract honesty.
**What they do poorly:** Reviews-only. Reporting is shallow. No conditional automation logic. No social, no voice, no marketing strategy.
**AI features specifically:** Pro tier has AI replies, booking reminders, referral campaigns, competitor insights. That's the whole AI surface.
**Reputation:** Solid mid-tier review tool, well-liked, never positioned as "the OS."
**Gap they leave:** Same operator, same wallet, but only review automation. HeyMaya is the natural upsell narrative ("you already pay $125 for review replies — for $99 more get the full marketing manager").

#### GatherUp
**Target:** SMB + agencies.
**Pricing:** ~$99–$350/mo, custom; pricing transparency is a published complaint.
**What they do well:** Solid review collection + survey logic.
**What they do poorly:** Pricing opacity. Reportedly degrading support quality. No real AI layer.
**Gap they leave:** Same as NiceJob — narrow review tool, ripe for operator to want "and also social/voice/strategy."

#### ReviewBuzz
**Target:** Home services specifically. 1-truck to 25-tech.
**Pricing:** $99/mo annual prepaid ($199/mo on monthly), plus $20/field employee/mo.
**What they do well:** Tech-by-tech leaderboard and gamification — incentivizes the field crew to ask for reviews. Not nothing.
**What they do poorly:** Per-tech pricing scales painfully. No AI worth speaking of. Aging UX.
**Gap they leave:** Reviews only. The gamification angle is interesting but it's a single-feature wedge.

#### Reputation.com
**Target:** Fortune 1000, multi-location enterprise, regulated industries.
**Pricing:** $80/mo entry, but real enterprise deployments are 5-figure monthly.
**What they do well:** Best-in-class Genius Reputation Agent, predictive analytics, SOC2/ISO compliance, multi-location intelligence.
**Wrong customer entirely** for HeyMaya's sandbox. Worth knowing for landscape completeness; not a meaningful threat to a 5-truck plumber.

#### Swell CX
**Target:** Multi-location healthcare, dental groups.
**Wrong vertical.** Strong AI surveys/reviews stack but not built for trades.

### Tier 2 — CRM-built-in AI features

#### ServiceTitan Marketing Pro
**Target:** Mid-market ($2M–$50M+) trades that already pay $245–$398/tech/mo for ServiceTitan + $10–30k implementation.
**Pricing:** Add-on. Adding Marketing Pro + Phones Pro typically pushes a $3k/mo ServiceTitan bill to $4.5k–$5k/mo.
**What they do well:** Genuinely powerful — AI SMS agents, AI Voice Agent (separate add-on), Autopilot Library of pre-built campaign templates, Atlas Campaign Recommendations (rolling out summer 2026), call recording + lead scoring + ROI tracking. ServiceTitan owns the data, so the AI is grounded.
**What they do poorly:** **Locked to ServiceTitan customers** — meaning anyone <$2M revenue is excluded. Add-on creep is the #1 G2 complaint. Setup is enterprise-shaped. Operator still has to **run** campaigns; the AI doesn't text the owner saying "your bookings dipped 12% w/w in 78704, I'm running a $200 Google Local Services boost there, ok?"
**Trajectory:** Doubling down on agentic everything — Atlas, AI Agents, Phones Pro, Marketing Pro all converging.
**Gap they leave:** Anybody not on ServiceTitan. And on ServiceTitan, anybody who wants a single proactive voice instead of a bundle of 4 Pro modules.

#### Housecall Pro AI Team
**Target:** SMB home services (1–25 trucks) on HCP. Direct overlap with HeyMaya's customer.
**Pricing:** Base $59–$329/user/mo. Marketing AI, CSR AI chat, Coach AI, Analyst AI, Help AI all **included free across all tiers**. 24/7 CSR voice answering is a paid add-on.
**What they do well:** Best free-AI-bundle in the SMB FSM space. Marketing AI sends the post-job review request. CSR AI Chat does web-chat triage. Coach AI gives operators ops nudges. Recently added Accountant AI ("ask your books a question").
**What they do poorly:** **AI lives inside the HCP dashboard** — it's not a voice that texts the owner proactively. Marketing AI is feature-flavored, not manager-flavored. Nothing about social media or brand voice. Very thin on outbound marketing strategy. And it's locked to HCP customers.
**Trajectory:** HCP is shipping AI features fast (Feb 2026 release dropped Accountant AI + automation triggers). They are the most aggressive SMB FSM in AI right now.
**Gap they leave:** A proactive AI manager that lives **outside** the FSM dashboard, in the operator's text thread, that owns the marketing/reputation/social brain — and works for the 40% of HCP customers who would never open the marketing tab.

#### Jobber AI
**Target:** SMB home/commercial services on Jobber ($29–$599/mo base).
**Pricing:** Bundled in core; AI Receptionist $99/mo add-on; Marketing Suite $79/mo add-on. Real bill scales fast.
**What they do well:** Smart timing on review asks (close, visit complete, or invoice paid — operator's choice). 1-click AI replies on Google reviews. Voice-powered actions inside the app. Personalized coaching ("Jobber learns how a business runs and recommends automations").
**What they do poorly:** Marketing Suite + AI Receptionist quickly stack to $200/mo above base. Same fundamental shape as HCP — AI as feature, not as a manager voice. No social. Locked to Jobber.
**Trajectory:** Jobber is investing in AI parity with HCP. Solid feature shipping cadence.
**Gap they leave:** Same as HCP — no proactive cross-channel manager living in the operator's text thread.

#### FieldEdge
**Target:** Mid-market home services.
**AI features:** **None.** Multiple sources confirm zero AI features in 2026. They are losing the AI battle to HCP/Jobber/ServiceTitan loudly.
**Gap they leave:** Their entire AI surface area. FieldEdge customers are an actively-poachable market.

### Tier 3 — Generic social/marketing tools used by service businesses

#### Hootsuite
**Target:** Mid-market + agencies. **Has explicitly moved upmarket and killed its free tier.**
**Pricing:** $99/mo (annual) / $149/mo (monthly) Standard, then up. No free.
**What they do well:** OwlyWriter (captions, blog-to-post, holiday ideas), OwlyGPT, image gen beta, brand voice personalization. Multi-account scheduling at scale.
**What they do poorly:** Generic. No service-business knowledge. Solo operators have been priced out and abandoned ("priced out intentionally" per multiple reviews). Operator still has to come up with the strategy and concept; Hootsuite just executes.
**Gap they leave:** Service-business-specific brain. Plus solo operators they explicitly walked away from.

#### Buffer
**Target:** Solopreneur, micro-SMB. The friendliest social pricing.
**Pricing:** Free tier (3 channels + AI), $5/channel Essentials, $10/channel Team.
**What they do well:** Free unlimited AI Assistant. Cheap channels 1–10. Very fast UX.
**What they do poorly:** AI output is "formal, generic, first-draft" — operators report needing 5–10 min refinement per post. No service-business voice. Becomes expensive past 10 channels. Just a scheduler — no strategist.
**Gap they leave:** A voice that knows your brand, your jobs, your reviews, and your local market.

#### Sprout Social
**Target:** Enterprise / mid-market.
**Pricing:** $249–$499/seat/mo.
**Wrong customer.** Service-business operator can't and shouldn't pay $249/seat for a social tool. Listed for completeness.

#### Loomly
**Pricing:** $42–$369/mo. Notable $267/mo gap between Starter and Beyond — known weakness.
**AI:** Caption *suggestions* only — no full generation. Calendar-first.
**Gap:** No real AI strategist.

#### Later
**Pricing:** $25 / $50 / $110/mo. AI metered by credits.
**Target:** Visual creators and IG-first brands. Wrong shape for plumbers.

#### Sendible
**Pricing:** $29 entry → $315/mo Advanced → $790/mo Enterprise.
**Target:** Agencies + small business. White-label capable.

#### MeetEdgar

## The HeyMaya wedge

**What no other competitor does, and we will:**

1. **A single proactive AI voice in the operator's text thread.** Not a dashboard. Not a feature in a CRM. Not a webchat widget. Maya pushes — morning brief, post-job review automation cited to the actual job, slow-week alert with a recommended Google LSA boost, a competitor's new ad copied to your inbox. **Birdeye, Podium, ServiceTitan, HCP, Jobber all require the operator to log in.** Hatch and Avoca push but only on inbound leads.

2. **Cross-channel manager scope at SMB price.** Reviews + social + brand voice + lead follow-up + accountability + competitor watch + revenue snapshot — for $99–$499/mo. The only existing way to get this scope is ServiceTitan + Marketing Pro + Phones Pro + an outside agency = $1.5k–$5k+/mo, locked to ServiceTitan, requires implementation.

3. **CRM-agnostic.** Works with ServiceTitan, HCP, Jobber, FieldEdge, **or no CRM at all**. Every meaningful AI player today (Marketing Pro, HCP AI Team, Jobber AI, Avoca, Arch) is welded to a single FSM. The 60% of <10-truck operators not on a major FSM are unserved.

4. **Service-business-trained voice from day one.** Hootsuite/Buffer/Loomly write generic captions. Maya knows that "summer HVAC capacity" is a marketing window, that a 5-star review from a senior citizen is gold, that a 1-star review at 11 PM is probably the one you respond to first thing in the morning, not at 11 PM. This is `playbook.md` + `cron.md` + `skill.md` shipped from day one.

5. **Grounded or silent.** Every recommendation cites the data — last 30 jobs, your reviews, your local Google Maps competitors. **Birdeye's "AI insights" are vague trend cards. ServiceTitan Atlas is opaque. Hootsuite captions hallucinate brand details.** HeyMaya cites or shuts up.

6. **Flat pricing, no theater.** Goodcall and Numa pioneered "AI = ~$99–$249/mo for an SMB" — we slot into that mental model. No per-location, no per-tech, no per-call, no add-on creep. **Birdeye per-location and Jobber/HCP add-on stacking are the operator pain we exploit.**

**Where competitors already do part of what we claim:**
- **Podium's "Jerry"** is a real proactive AI lead-handler — but only for inbound leads, not for outbound marketing/reviews/social. Honest read: Podium is the closest analog to "AI employee voice" today. But they're SMS-funnel-only and $400–$1000/mo.
- **HCP's Coach AI** does push some operator nudges. But it's inside the HCP app, not a text thread, and it's pretty thin.
- **Birdeye's AI agents** are real — but reactive, not proactive, and per-location-priced.

So the wedge is honest: nobody owns "proactive cross-channel AI manager living in the operator's text thread for $99–$499/mo, CRM-agnostic, service-business-trained."

| 10+ truck on ServiceTitan | ServiceTitan ~$2,500/mo + Marketing Pro + Phones Pro + Avoca = **$4,000–$7,000/mo** |
| Any size + agency | + Scorpion/Blue Corona retainer $2,000–$10,000/mo |

### Validating the proposed $99 / $249 / $499 Maya pricing

- **$99 (Starter)** sits cleanly between **NiceJob Pro ($125)** and **Goodcall Starter ($79)** — operator's mental anchor for "one piece of AI tooling" is $79–$150. **$99 is in the comfort zone.** Should be more than NiceJob alone because we do more.
- **$249 (Pro)** sits at **Birdeye Starter ($299)** and **Goodcall Scale ($249)** — operator's "real software budget" tier. Defensible if pitched as "replaces NiceJob + Buffer + your agency's monthly check-in call."
- **$499 (Studio)** is **half of Hatch (~$900)** and **a third of Avoca/Arch (~$1,500)**. Should land as "your ops director" — multi-platform, brand voice across the whole house, brand outreach, contract review.

**Watch-outs:**
- Podium's $399 Core / $599 Pro normalizes 4-figure SMB tools. We can probably charge $299 mid-tier instead of $249 without flinching, especially given Podium's no-social gap.
- Birdeye's per-location creep means a 3-location operator pays them $900–$1,300/mo. We should explicitly **not** per-location price — flat is the wedge.
- ServiceTitan customers expect $1k+/mo line items. The $499 Studio tier could realistically be $699 or even $999 for that segment, sold as a ServiceTitan add-on bundle.

## Marketing angles to test

Five distinct landing-page narratives to A/B:

1. **"Your AI marketing manager. Before you can afford a human one."** — Direct mirror of the creator product positioning. Speaks to the 1–10 truck operator who knows they should have a marketing person but can't justify $5k/mo. **Differentiator: scope + price.**

2. **"Stop logging into 6 apps. Maya texts you."** — Stack-collapse pitch. Lead with the comparison: NiceJob + Buffer + Birdeye + Hatch + Scorpion = $1,500+/mo. Maya = $249/mo, in your messages. **Differentiator: UX (chat, not dashboards) + cost.**

3. **"She knows your jobs. So her marketing actually works."** — Grounded-AI pitch. Show side-by-side: Hootsuite caption ("Summer is here, beat the heat!") vs Maya caption ("That capacitor we replaced for the Hendersons in Cedar Park yesterday — 6 of your last 12 jobs in 78613 were the same fix. Here's a post about it tagged for that ZIP."). **Differentiator: data-grounded vs generic.**

4. **"Don't replace your CRM. Augment it."** — CRM-agnostic pitch for the FieldEdge refugees, the no-CRM operators, and the HCP/Jobber operators tired of paying for the AI add-on stack. **Differentiator: integration breadth.**

5. **"The proactive layer ServiceTitan won't sell you."** — High-end pitch targeting ServiceTitan operators specifically. Frames Maya as the layer **on top of** ServiceTitan that does what Marketing Pro can't — proactive voice in the operator's text thread, not a campaign to log in and approve. **Differentiator: Mode (push vs pull).**

## Strongest competitor we should be most concerned about

**Housecall Pro.** Two reasons:
1. **They serve our exact target customer** (1–25 truck SMB) and they are **shipping AI features the fastest** of any FSM. Marketing AI, CSR AI Chat, Coach AI, Analyst AI, Help AI — all included free. Their Feb 2026 release added Accountant AI and granular automation triggers. They own the customer relationship and the data; bolting an AI manager voice on top is a quarter of work for them.
2. **The natural competitive response to HeyMaya is for HCP to ship "HCP Maya" as a chat-thread experience** — and they have the engineering resources and the customer base to do it.

Mitigation thesis: **CRM-agnostic + service-business-specialized voice + the proactive-text-thread interaction model**, executed faster and tighter than HCP can ship inside a 200-engineer org. Plus the upsell narrative for **non-HCP operators** (ServiceTitan customers, Jobber customers, FieldEdge refugees, no-CRM operators) is a moat HCP can't easily cross.

**Runners-up to watch:**
- **Podium** — Jerry is the closest existing thing to "AI employee voice." If they extend Jerry from inbound-lead-handling into outbound marketing/reviews, the wedge narrows. They've signaled they will.
- **ServiceTitan Atlas** — when it ships GA (summer 2026), it will absorb a lot of "proactive marketing AI" mindshare in mid-market trades. Doesn't reach our SMB segment, but it sets buyer expectations.
- **Avoca + Arch combo on ServiceTitan** — if they integrate, they cover voice + marketing for ST customers, which is a real "manager-shaped" stack at $2k+/mo.
