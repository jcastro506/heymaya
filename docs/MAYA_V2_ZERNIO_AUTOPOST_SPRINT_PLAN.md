# Maya GTM v2 — "She Runs Your Growth and Posts For You": The Single-$99, Zernio-Powered, Auto-Post End-to-End Sprint Plan

> Architect synthesis from a 6-agent research fan-out over the live Zernio docs + our codebase, 2026-06-02. Builds on docs/ZERNIO_INTEGRATION_PLAN.md.

## Vision
Maya stops being a link generator and becomes a closed-loop growth operator. Today she researches where a founder's buyers live, drafts in their voice, and hands over one-tap deep links the founder pastes by hand — because per-platform app-approval walls made true auto-post impossible. Zernio (getlate.dev's successor; one app-level ZERNIO_API_KEY + per-founder OAuth-as-a-service across 15 platforms) holds the platform approvals itself, collapsing that wall. So Maya now does the full loop: research the product + ICP + where they live → deliver a plain-language plan in chat naming her ~4 bet channels and how she'll run each → the founder says "go" → Maya texts Zernio connect links via Telegram → the founder taps through hosted OAuth → and from the next morning Maya plans the whole day AND POSTS IT. Reads (GET /v1/analytics, follower-stats, inbox) ground "what actually converted" beyond our wrapped-link clicks; webhooks (post.published, comment/message/mention.received, account.disconnected) make her event-driven — she tracks a post the moment it lands, drafts voice-matched replies to inbound, and nudges a reconnect before "we post for you" silently breaks.

The product becomes ONE $99 plan: "Maya runs your growth end-to-end, driven by your real numbers," with a Studio tier above for power users. The two load-bearing moats are non-negotiable and NEVER tier-gated: ban-safety (Reddit + TikTok stay manual/confirm-to-post even when connected — two independent reasons: account risk AND Zernio's own docs admit >50% of Reddit posts fail) and attribution (wrapped-link clicks stay Maya's primary same-day signal; Zernio analytics are the slower 60min–48h ground-truth confirmation). The Google Calendar flood retires — the day's plan becomes Maya's internal auto-post execution queue rendering in a web "Today" view; the phone (Telegram) only buzzes for things needing a TAP. Everything is interface-isolated behind convex/integrations/zernio so a vendor swap (Postiz/Ayrshare) stays under two weeks.

## End-to-end flow
STEP 1 — SIGNUP + INTAKE (app/onboarding/gtm/page.tsx, intake stage; sub-4-min, unchanged-or-lighter). Founder signs up via Clerk and fills the existing one-page intake: name/url/appType/conversionKind/signupUrl/founderWhy/stage/entryMode/weekGoal/userCountBand + existing-handle URL fields (still drive ScrapeCreators voice extraction + per-channel warmth) + capability toggles. REMOVE all Google-Calendar mental-model copy. Do NOT add connect buttons here — connect is deferred until after the plan sells the founder.

STEP 2 — RESEARCH (foundation-research + continuous-research). Maya researches the product + ICP + where they live + what they watch/say/complain. This produces snapshot.channelScores + icpKnowledge. No founder action.

STEP 2.5 — ICP CONFIRM IN CHAT (no onboarding form field — decided 2026-06-02). We do NOT add a "who's this for?" intake field (keeps intake zero-bloat; we already ask `excludedAudiences` = who to AVOID, and Maya's grounded research carries the positive ICP). Instead Maya INFERS the buyer map from research, then OPENS the synthesis by presenting it back as a CONFIRMABLE CLAIM before the channel plan: a specific, cited one-liner ("Here's who I think actually buys this and where they live — solo iOS devs frustrated with X, in r/iOSProgramming + #buildinpublic on X. That match your read, or am I off?"). This is grounded (shows the research, not a black box), gives a cheap one-reply correction point, and lets Maya PUSH BACK with evidence when the founder is wrong ("you said enterprise, but everyone complaining about this is a solo dev — here are 8 threads") — anti-sycophancy doing real work. A correction in the founder's reply WRITES BACK into the buyer map (upsertBuyerMap + per-channel icpKnowledge) and, if the ICP shifts materially, re-grounds the channel scores before the plan card renders. Implementation: foundation-research SKILL synthesis-open carries the ICP-confirm beat; the inbound reply handler routes an ICP correction to the buyer-map write-back, not just a chat ack.

STEP 3 — HIGH-LEVEL PLAN IN CHAT (replaces the channel-picker grid, page.tsx lines 817-900). After the ICP-confirm beat (Step 2.5), Maya renders a HIGH-LEVEL PLAN card from the foundation synthesis: a short plain-language paragraph ("Here's how I'll get you customers") + EXACTLY her recommended ~4 bet channels, each a row {channel name, one-line 'why — your buyers live here' from icpKnowledge, one-line 'how I'll run it' e.g. "X: daily build-in-public replies, I post these for you" / "Reddit: I draft, you tap to post — ban-safety" / "LinkedIn: 3x/week, auto-posted"}. The SAME plan text goes to Telegram as the synthesis message (foundation-research SKILL close rewritten: DROP "drop today's move onto your Google Calendar"; ADD "Here are your ~4 channels + how I'll run each. Say go and I'll text you the connect links — tap each one, and from tomorrow I plan AND post your whole day."). Default action = single primary button "Go — run my growth" (an 'edit channels' affordance stays for power users; default is accept-Maya's-bet).

STEP 4 — AGREE → TELEGRAM CONNECT LINKS (new outbound message type). On "go", Maya sends ONE Telegram message listing the ~4 channels, each a tappable Zernio connect deep link from getZernioConnectUrl(platform) → GET /v1/connect/{platform}?profileId&redirect_url. First connect creates the founder's Zernio profile (POST /v1/profiles, persist zernioProfileId) and registers the webhook (POST /webhooks/create-webhook-settings). All 6 offered channels use the uniform hosted-OAuth window (no app-password branch — Bluesky is not offered, so the "Maya never sees your password" framing holds for every channel).

STEP 5 — ZERNIO HOSTED OAUTH. Founder taps → Zernio-hosted OAuth + account-picker (third-party; Maya's message sets expectations: "this opens Zernio's secure login — I never see your password") → redirect to PUBLIC /lc_gtm/zernio_callback?connected&accountId&profileId&username. The callback binds accountId → the correct gtmAgent via a signed/nonce'd profileId (cross-tenant isolation), persists into connectedAccountsJson on gtmAgents, and detects IG-personal/FB-Page/LinkedIn-personal gaps inline ("your IG is a personal account — I can't auto-post there until you switch to Business; want the 2-step how-to?"). After each callback Maya texts "TikTok connected as @x — 3 of 4 done"; on the last, "All set — I'll plan and post your first full day tomorrow morning."

STEP 6 — AUTO-RUN THE DAY. Next morning the morning_brief cron BUILDS the day → writes gtmCalendarEvents rows: auto-postable post kinds (soft_launch_post/hard_launch_anchor) on connected low-risk channels written status='queued'; Reddit/TikTok + reply/engagement/draft items written status='draft'/'needs_confirm'. Queued rows fire scheduled Zernio posts (scheduledFor = startsAtMs ISO, firstComment for the wrapped link); a 24h confirmEventLanded re-poll flips 'posting'→'published' only after verification. Telegram gets the once-daily brief ("6 posts going out for you, 2 need your tap — they're in Today") + only TAP items (draft approvals, Reddit/TikTok confirm cards, hot inbound replies, reconnect nudges). Everything else renders silently in the web Today view. If the founder never connected, Today degrades gracefully to the OLD "I drafted these, tap to post" deep-link mode (fallback, not a dead end) and a reconnect-nudge cadence fires.

## Discovery cadence (the pulse loop)
With auto-post live, discovery must be a regular heartbeat, not the lopsided 7am+1pm-then-blind-all-afternoon model. The cron set:
- **7am operator-local — `morning_brief` (the deep one).** Calls `get_my_foundation` (stored buyer map + per-channel `icpKnowledge` + `channelWarmthJson` — does NOT re-derive the ICP), runs the FULL deep sweep across all bet channels, then builds + queues TODAY's plan (sharpened by `get_my_attribution`). This is itself the day's deepest pulse.
- **Every ~4h through waking hours (~11am / 3pm / 7pm) — `pulse` (light, fresh-only).** Generalizes the old single `midday_pulse` into a recurring catch-before-peak sweep. For each NEWLY-surfaced thread Maya judges "is this worth my founder dropping something witty/sharp/useful here, in their voice, for their brand?" — if yes, draft in-voice + ADD a one-tap (or auto-post) event to today; if nothing clears the bar, stay silent. Covers the afternoon/evening engagement peaks the 2x model missed. Bounded to waking hours (no 3am pulse — the 7am sweep catches overnight). Catches everything except a sub-4h viral spike (would need hourly polling / a trending API — out of v1 scope; name the limit, don't silently cap).
- **8pm evening recap (conditional, no discovery); Sun 7pm weekly review (strategic, no discovery); heartbeat 5/30min (monitor-only, NEVER discovers).**
- **Cost discipline (load-bearing):** each pulse is fresh-only (filter to `gtmTargetThreads/gtmNichePulse.observedAt` newer than last sweep) and ADAPTIVE — check observedAt + early-stop if nothing's newer / a channel's quiet, so a dead afternoon costs ~nothing. A light pulse is ~¼ a full sweep, so 7am-full + 3 light ≈ 1.75 sweep-equivalents/day (vs ~1.25 today) — modest, within $99 margin ONLY if the fresh-only/early-stop discipline holds. Later: snap pulse windows to each channel's actual peak via `get_platform_algo` timing instead of a rigid 4h clock.
- Code: cron registration in `convex/agents/packs/maya_gtm/generators.ts` (BOOT step 4 + the "What I do, day by day" cron list) — generalize `midday_pulse` → recurring `pulse`; cadence section in `agents/skills/maya-gtm/maya-continuous-research/SKILL.md`.

## Dedup — the engagement ledger (never comment twice on the same thing)
Autonomous posting every 4h is exactly where a double-comment bug bites, so dedup must be a hard server-side guarantee, NOT prompt discipline.
- **What already exists (thread-level idempotency, keep it):** `gtmTargetThreads` is deduped on `(accountId, platform, externalId)` (externalId = the platform's own post id) — `saveTargetThread` UPSERTS (re-found thread patches the existing row, never double-inserts; status is preserved on patch, not reset to queued). Status lifecycle `queued|replied|dropped|expired`: on publish, the thread flips to `replied` + links `draftedReplyId`. Drafts carry `targetThreadId`. So the same thread is ONE row across all pulses.
- **The gaps to close:** (1) skipping `replied` threads is currently prompt-only — nothing REFUSES to publish a second reply to an already-`replied` thread; (2) no comment-level granularity (externalId is the thread, not the specific comment — a thread has many `mineableComments[]`); (3) `gtmPostResults` records `platform`+`providerPostId` only, not the thread it replied to, so "what have we actually posted to?" isn't queryable.
- **THE DESIGN — Convex ledger, enforced fail-closed (decided):**
  - **Authority lives in Convex, NOT OpenClaw memory.** OpenClaw session memory is per-session + volatile (the 7am cron and the 3pm pulse can run in different sessions on a restarted machine), so it cannot be the dedup authority. Convex is the only thing every cron shares and it survives restarts; OpenClaw just ASKS Convex.
  - **Defense in depth, two layers.** PROMPT layer (cheap, avoids wasted drafting): a new typed tool `check_already_engaged({platform, externalId, commentId?})` Maya calls before drafting + the pulse queries `getMyTargetThreads({status:'queued'})` only. SERVER layer (the real guarantee): the `zernio_post` / `publishQueuedEvent` route REFUSES to publish a reply whose `(agentId, platform, externalId[, commentId])` is already engaged — unless an explicit `intentionalFollowUp:true` flag is set — mirroring the fail-closed ban-safety gate. Model drift cannot double-post.
  - **Comment granularity with NO new table** (schema at the TS DataModel ceiling): add `targetExternalId:v.optional(v.string())` + `targetCommentId:v.optional(v.string())` to `gtmPostResults` so the actual-posted record is keyed to exactly what we replied to; the publish gate reads that + thread.status. Reuse the existing `(accountId, platform)` index + inline externalId filter (same pattern as `saveTargetThread`'s dedupe scan) — no new index needed.
  - Net guarantee: ONE reply per thread (or per specific comment) ever, enforced in the one place all crons share, even if the LLM forgets.
- Code: `convex/gtmMaya/targetList.ts` (the `check_already_engaged` query + keep the upsert), the `zernio_post`/`publishQueuedEvent` publish gate (S3), `gtmPostResults` schema fields, and a `maya-publisher`/`maya-engagement-responder` skill note ("always `check_already_engaged` before drafting a reply; the server enforces it anyway").
