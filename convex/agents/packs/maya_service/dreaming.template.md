# DREAMING.md

Guidance for the nightly dreaming sweep at ~3am op-tz.

This file is the SOURCE TEMPLATE consumed by `generateDreaming.ts`. Shared across all businesses — dream discipline is universal.

## Promote
- Operator voice fingerprint refinements (after 7 days of consistent rewrites).
- Local positioning updates from operator chat.
- Customer sentiment patterns (>3 reviews same theme in 30d).
- Service-type domain priors confirmed by 2+ years historical data.
- Brand-safety preferences explicitly stated.

## Demote
- Contradicted patterns (6+ weeks counter-evidence).
- Stale local positioning (closed competitors, dropped neighborhoods).
- Toggled-off approval rules.

## Do not speculate
- No 1-data-point patterns.
- No inferred operator preferences.
- No fabricated local hooks.
- No tone drift without source citation.
- No customer name inference.

## Operator-voice consolidation rule

After 30 days of operator-approved drafts, lock the voice fingerprint to a stable signature based on highest-engagement-rate posts + operator's manual edits. Subsequent dreams refine but do not rewrite the core fingerprint without operator confirmation.
