Domain priors for electrical service businesses. Loaded as the curated MEMORY.md initial seed when `business.serviceTypes` includes `electrical`.

<!-- section:domain_priors:start -->
## Domain priors

- **Customer concerns rank in this order:** safety (no shocks, no fires) > licensing/certifications visible > code-compliance > price.
- **Licensing matters more than for any other trade.** Reviews mentioning "licensed" or "master electrician" cluster at 5 stars; reviews from unlicensed-feeling jobs cluster at 1-2.
- **Permit + inspection** is the silent quality signal. Operators who pull permits routinely get "everything was up to code, inspector signed off" reviews.
- **Panel upgrades + EV chargers** are the discretionary high-ticket spend. Both require permits + inspections; never imply otherwise.
- **Generators (whole-home, transfer switch)** are seasonal upsells around weather events.

## Seasonal calendar

- **Pre-storm season (varies by region):** generator + surge-protection content. Hurricane season (Atlantic), tornado season (Midwest), ice-storm season (Northeast).
- **Holiday lighting (November-December):** outdoor outlet + lighting safety. GFCI checks.
- **Summer (June-August):** AC/EV-charger circuit upgrades (paired with HVAC peak).
- **Spring (March-May):** outdoor lighting, landscape lighting, hot-tub circuits.
<!-- section:seasonal_calendar:end -->

<!-- section:brand_safety:start -->
## Brand-safety memory

- **Never imply work is permit-free.** "Quick outlet swap, no permit needed" is a code-compliance trap. Always defer permit questions to the operator.
- **Never quote panel-upgrade pricing.** Variables: amperage, distance from meter, sub-panels, weather-head replacement. Quote = on-site only.
- **Never advise on DIY safety questions** in chat ("is it safe to replace this myself?"). "I'd want a licensed eye on it — happy to schedule a free assessment."
- **Generator load calculations** require licensed sizing — never recommend a generator size off a phone call.
- **EV charger compatibility** ("will my Tesla work with this?") requires the operator's product spec — don't guess.
<!-- section:brand_safety:end -->

<!-- section:review_reply_patterns:start -->
## Review reply patterns (electrical-specific)

- **5-star with safety praise:** lead with the safety fact ("permit pulled, inspection passed"); reinforce licensing as the moat.
- **5-star, technician named:** thank by name + cite the certification level if appropriate.
- **3-star with price complaint:** "Electrical work is permit + licensed-time + materials; I'd be happy to walk through the line items — please call." Never argue billing in public.
- **1-star with safety concern:** flag for operator-only. Safety reviews are existential — never auto-draft.
