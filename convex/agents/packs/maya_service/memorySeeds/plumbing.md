# MEMORY.md seed — Plumbing

Domain priors for plumbing service businesses. Loaded as the curated MEMORY.md initial seed when `business.serviceTypes` includes `plumbing`.

<!-- section:domain_priors:start -->
## Domain priors

- **Customer concerns rank in this order:** speed (water on the floor = panic) > pricing transparency upfront > technician cleanliness > guarantee/warranty terms.
- **Reviews almost always mention:** how fast the operator answered the phone, whether the price quoted matched the price billed, whether the leak was fully fixed, technician's manners.
- **Flat-rate vs T&M pricing model** matters — operator's preference goes in MEMORY.md after first observation. Customers who got a flat-rate quote talk about value; customers who got T&M talk about minutes.
- **Emergency vs scheduled** is the volume split. Emergency commands 1.5-2× scheduled rates; never quote a number.
- **Same-day service** is the usual close. "Can you come today?" → "let me check the schedule with the operator now" → CRM lookup → operator confirms.
<!-- section:domain_priors:end -->

<!-- section:seasonal_calendar:start -->
## Seasonal calendar

- **Freeze season (December-February):** burst-pipe surge. Lead-response alarms tightest. Pre-freeze content: pipe insulation, drip-faucet reminders, where the main shutoff is.
- **Spring thaw (March-April):** sump-pump and basement-flood content. Drainage check upsells.
- **Summer (June-August):** outdoor faucet, garden-hose-bib repairs, water-heater swaps (highest discretionary spend window).
- **Fall (September-November):** winterize content — outdoor shutoffs, frost-free hose-bibs, pre-freeze inspections.
<!-- section:seasonal_calendar:end -->

## Brand-safety memory

- **Never quote pricing in chat.** "What does a water heater install cost?" gets "depends on tankless vs tank, gas vs electric, location — want me to set up a free quote?" Operator owns pricing.
- **Never schedule without confirmation.** Customer urgency ("we have water everywhere") prompts an immediate operator ping, not a Maya-side commitment.
- **Backflow + permits** always go through the operator. Backflow re-certification is regulated by jurisdiction; do not imply scheduling without operator owning the permit/cert paperwork.
- **Code questions** ("is this up to code?") get "I'd want the operator to look at it before I answer — want me to set up an inspection?"
- **Insurance claims** (water damage from a burst) require operator handling — never advise a customer on what their insurance will or won't cover.
<!-- section:brand_safety:end -->

## Review reply patterns (plumbing-specific)

- **5-star, fast-response praise:** lead with the response time fact ("we got out same day, glad we could help"); don't gush.
- **5-star, technician-named:** thank by name + mention specifically what was fixed.
- **3-star or below with pricing complaint:** never get into the bill in public. "I'd like to look at the invoice with you — please call us" + sign off.
- **Emergency-room-quality reviews** (high-stakes outage, customer panicked): empathy first, fix-confirmation second. Never minimize.
