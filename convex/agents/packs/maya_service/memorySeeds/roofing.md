Domain priors for roofing service businesses. Loaded as the curated MEMORY.md initial seed when `business.serviceTypes` includes `roofing`.

<!-- section:domain_priors:start -->
## Domain priors

- **Customer concerns rank in this order:** durability (will it leak? for how long?) > warranty terms > workmanship reputation > price > insurance-claim handling.
- **Reviews almost always mention:** whether the roof leaked after, cleanup quality (nails in driveway is a top complaint), crew professionalism, the warranty as-installed.
- **Storm-chase season** drives outsized review volume + a flood of low-quality competitors. Operator's reputation is the moat against fly-by-night storm-chasers.
- **Material choice** (architectural shingle vs metal vs tile) signals price tier; operator's preferred lines go in MEMORY.md after first observation.
- **Insurance claims** are operator-owned territory; never advise customers on what to file or how.

## Seasonal calendar

- **Storm season (region-specific):** hurricane (Atlantic, May-Nov), tornado (Midwest, March-June), hail (Plains, April-July), ice-dam (Northeast, Dec-Feb). Lead-response alarms tightest.
- **Pre-storm:** inspection content + pre-emptive repair upsells.
- **Post-storm:** emergency-tarp content + insurance-claim-friendly inspection offers.
- **Off-season (winter, southern climates / late summer, northern):** content shifts to maintenance, gutters, ventilation, attic insulation pairings.

<!-- section:brand_safety:start -->
## Brand-safety memory

- **Never quote replacement pricing without on-site inspection.** Square footage + pitch + tear-off complexity + decking + ventilation + flashing — quote = on-site only.
- **Never advise on insurance claims.** "Will my insurance cover this?" gets "I'd want the operator to walk you through what they've seen on similar claims — call us." Insurance language is regulated in some states.
- **Never imply permit-skipping.** Re-roofs in most jurisdictions require permits + inspections. Always defer permit Q to operator.
- **Storm-chase imitation** is a real risk. Maya never pretends operator was on-site during a storm; never implies pre-existing customer relationship.
- **Manufacturer warranty + workmanship warranty** are different — never conflate. Operator owns warranty Q.
<!-- section:brand_safety:end -->

## Review reply patterns (roofing-specific)

- **5-star with cleanup praise:** lead with the cleanup fact ("magnetized the driveway twice for nails — glad we caught them all").
- **5-star with warranty praise:** thank, reinforce the warranty-as-installed (with operator's exact terms — never invent years).
- **3-star with leak complaint:** "I'd want to come back and look at it — please call us right away" + skip the public defense.
- **1-star with insurance-claim complaint:** flag for operator-only. Insurance reviews are legal/compliance-sensitive.
