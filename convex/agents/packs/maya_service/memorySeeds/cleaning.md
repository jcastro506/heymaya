# MEMORY.md seed — Cleaning

Domain priors for residential + commercial cleaning service businesses. Loaded as the curated MEMORY.md initial seed when `business.serviceTypes` includes `cleaning`.

<!-- section:domain_priors:start -->
## Domain priors

- **Customer concerns rank in this order:** trust (in their home / business unattended) > consistency (same crew, same standard, every time) > thoroughness > price.
- **Reviews almost always mention:** the cleaner by name, attention to detail (specific items the cleaner moved/cleaned), reliability, communication.
- **Recurring is the business model.** One-time deep cleans are entry points; weekly/biweekly recurring is the LTV anchor. Maya treats churn signals (skipped weeks, schedule changes) as urgent.
- **Photos are sensitive.** Customer homes are private; photo content is rare and operator-curated. Commercial cleaning has more flexibility but still operator-owned.
- **Eco/green vs traditional** segmentation matters; operator's product line goes in MEMORY.md after first observation.
<!-- section:domain_priors:end -->

<!-- section:seasonal_calendar:start -->
## Seasonal calendar

- **Spring (March-May):** spring cleaning surge. Deep-clean upsells.
- **Pre-summer (April-June):** move-in/move-out cleanings (rental turnovers).
- **Pre-holiday (October-December):** holiday-prep deep cleans.
- **Post-holiday (January):** reset cleanings, new-year-new-routine inquiries.
- **Steady-state:** recurring service is year-round; content cadence stays steady.
<!-- section:seasonal_calendar:end -->

<!-- section:brand_safety:start -->
## Brand-safety memory

- **Never quote recurring pricing without operator approval.** Square footage + frequency + product preference + special requests — operator owns it.
- **Privacy is paramount.** Never name a customer in public content without explicit permission. Commercial cleaning requires owner-of-business permission. Residential cleaning almost never gets named publicly.
- **Damage claims** (broken vase, scratched floor) require operator handling — empathy in public, resolution in private.
- **Trust + key-management** ("you have a key to my house") requires operator-owned policy. Maya does not discuss key/access protocols in chat.
- **Cleaner background-check signaling** is a moat ("all cleaners background-checked + insured") — surface it consistently in posts when operator opts in.
<!-- section:brand_safety:end -->

## Review reply patterns (cleaning-specific)

- **5-star with cleaner-named:** thank by name, note the specific detail mentioned ("she always remembers the dog's water bowl").
- **5-star recurring praise:** thank with frequency reference ("every Tuesday for two years now").
- **3-star with consistency complaint:** "I'd like to make sure your next clean is exactly what you expect — please call" + offer a free re-clean.
- **1-star with damage or trust concern:** flag for operator-only. Trust reviews require operator handling.
