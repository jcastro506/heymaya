Domain priors for pest control service businesses. Loaded as the curated MEMORY.md initial seed when `business.serviceTypes` includes `pest`.

<!-- section:domain_priors:start -->
## Domain priors

- **Customer concerns rank in this order:** discretion (neighbors don't need to know) > speed > effectiveness (does it actually work) > pet/child safety > price.
- **Reviews almost always mention:** technician's manners + appearance (uniformed/branded), explanation of treatment, pet/child safety questions answered, reliability of follow-up visits.
- **Recurring (quarterly/monthly) is the business model.** One-time bug-out is the entry point.
- **Marketing photos are tricky.** Customers want effectiveness signals but don't want their address publicly tied to a "we treat their roach problem" post. Branded vehicles, clean uniforms, before/after of equipment — yes. Customer addresses — no.
- **Specialty pests** (termites, bed bugs, wildlife) are higher-stakes + higher-ticket; treat them differently from general pest.

<!-- section:seasonal_calendar:start -->
## Seasonal calendar

- **Spring (March-May):** ant + termite season. Pre-emptive treatment content. Termite-inspection upsells.
- **Summer (June-August):** mosquito + roach surge. Outdoor mosquito-treatment content peaks.
- **Fall (September-November):** rodent prep — mice/rats moving indoors. Wildlife exclusion (squirrels, raccoons in attics).
- **Winter (December-February):** indoor-pest content. Bed bug season is year-round but spikes around holiday travel.
<!-- section:seasonal_calendar:end -->

<!-- section:brand_safety:start -->
## Brand-safety memory

- **Never quote treatment pricing without operator approval.** Pest type + property size + severity + recurring vs one-time — operator owns it.
- **Discretion is paramount.** Never name a customer's address, neighborhood-specific to a single house, or pest type tied to an identifiable customer in public content.
- **Pesticide regulation** varies by jurisdiction. Never imply use of restricted products; never promise "kills 100%."
- **Pet/child safety** Q's get "the operator can walk you through which products are pet/child safe — let's set up a call."
- **Wildlife** (raccoons, squirrels, bats) often requires permits + humane-removal protocols; operator-only.
<!-- section:brand_safety:end -->

## Review reply patterns (pest-specific)

- **5-star with technician-named:** thank by name, note the specific detail ("explained everything clearly" / "showed me the entry points").
- **5-star recurring praise:** thank with frequency reference, never name the pest type ("third year with you").
- **3-star with effectiveness complaint:** "If you're still seeing them, I want us to come back — please call" + free re-treatment offer.
- **1-star with safety concern** (pet got sick, child got rash): flag for operator-only — pesticide-safety reviews are regulated territory.
