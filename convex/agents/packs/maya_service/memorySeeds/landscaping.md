# MEMORY.md seed — Landscaping

Domain priors for landscaping service businesses. Loaded as the curated MEMORY.md initial seed when `business.serviceTypes` includes `landscaping`.

<!-- section:domain_priors:start -->
## Domain priors

- **Customer concerns rank in this order:** aesthetics (does it look good) > reliability (do they show up) > price > equipment care (do they damage the lawn/sprinklers).
- **Reviews almost always mention:** before-and-after visual results, whether the crew showed up on the scheduled day, cleanup quality, the foreman's name.
- **Recurring vs one-time mix.** Recurring (mow + maintain) is steady revenue; one-time (install, hardscape, tree work) is spiky high-ticket. Maya treats them differently in content.
- **Visual content is mandatory.** A landscaping post without a photo is a wasted post. Before/after pairs outperform single shots 3-5×.
- **Crew named-foreman** is the trust anchor — customers ask for foremen by name after one good job.
<!-- section:domain_priors:end -->

<!-- section:seasonal_calendar:start -->
## Seasonal calendar

- **Spring (March-May):** clean-up, mulch refresh, aeration, overseeding. Highest content-output season — operator has the most material.
- **Summer (June-August):** maintenance routines, irrigation, bed-edging, weed control. Drought content if region-relevant.
- **Fall (September-November):** leaf cleanup, fall planting, pre-winter prep, lawn aeration.
- **Winter (December-February):** snow removal (where relevant), tree pruning, design-planning content for the next year. Slowest revenue period — content cadence may dip but doesn't go to zero.
<!-- section:seasonal_calendar:end -->

<!-- section:brand_safety:start -->
## Brand-safety memory

- **Never quote install pricing without operator approval.** Hardscape quotes are highly variable (materials, drainage, labor, equipment access).
- **Tree work has liability implications.** Never imply tree-removal scheduling without operator owning the safety + insurance angle. Tree-near-power-lines is operator-only.
- **Customer-supplied materials** ("can you install pavers I bought?") have warranty implications — defer to operator.
- **Pesticide/herbicide application** is regulated; never imply use of restricted products. Defer to operator's licensing.
- **HOA-specific work** ("can you make my front yard match the HOA spec?") needs operator review; don't promise compliance.
<!-- section:brand_safety:end -->

<!-- section:review_reply_patterns:start -->
## Review reply patterns (landscaping-specific)

- **5-star with photo or before/after praise:** thank, name the foreman if mentioned, note the season-specific work that was done.
- **5-star recurring-customer:** thank with year-count or season-count if known ("third spring with you — appreciate the trust").
- **3-star with reliability complaint:** "I'm sorry we missed the day — let's get you back on the schedule" + offer to make it right.
- **1-star with damage complaint** (sprinkler hit, tree damaged): flag for operator-only — never imply liability without operator owning the conversation.
