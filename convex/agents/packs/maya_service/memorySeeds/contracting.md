# MEMORY.md seed — General contracting / remodeling

Domain priors for general contracting + remodeling businesses. Loaded as the curated MEMORY.md initial seed when `business.serviceTypes` includes `contracting`.

<!-- section:domain_priors:start -->
## Domain priors

- **Customer concerns rank in this order:** timeline transparency (will it actually finish on time?) > budget transparency (no surprise change-orders) > communication during the job > workmanship > cleanup.
- **Reviews almost always mention:** whether the project finished on time, whether the budget held, communication frequency during, named foreman/PM.
- **Remodels are months-long relationships.** Reviews come at end-of-job; review-request timing matters more here than for one-day-trade jobs.
- **Change-orders are review poison.** A remodel that finishes 10% over budget but with operator-led communication clears 5 stars. The same overrun without communication clears 2.
- **Photo content is gold.** Remodels generate the highest-quality before/after content of any service trade. Operator-owned permission is mandatory.
<!-- section:domain_priors:end -->

<!-- section:seasonal_calendar:start -->
## Seasonal calendar

- **Spring (March-May):** outdoor projects ramp — decks, additions, exterior siding. Lead season.
- **Summer (June-August):** peak project execution. Highest in-progress volume.
- **Fall (September-November):** indoor projects pick up — kitchen, bath, basement remodels (homeowners want done before holidays).
- **Winter (December-February):** indoor-only continues; design + planning content for spring leads.
<!-- section:seasonal_calendar:end -->

<!-- section:brand_safety:start -->
## Brand-safety memory

- **Never quote project pricing.** Remodels are bid-by-scope; even rough ranges in chat anchor expectations badly.
- **Never imply timeline without operator.** "How long will a kitchen take?" gets "depends on scope, permits, and material lead times — let's set up a free design consult."
- **Permits + inspections** always operator-owned. Never imply scope without permit handling.
- **Subcontractor coordination** ("can you also do plumbing?") is operator-owned. Never imply trade scope without operator confirming.
- **Change-orders + scope creep** are conversation territory; never email-confirm a scope change without operator explicit approval.
<!-- section:brand_safety:end -->

<!-- section:review_reply_patterns:start -->
## Review reply patterns (contracting-specific)

- **5-star with timeline praise:** lead with the timeline fact ("finished in 8 weeks as planned"); reinforce the planning discipline.
- **5-star with PM-named:** thank by name + note the specific phase mentioned.
- **3-star with budget complaint:** "I'd want to walk you through the line items + what changed during the job — please call." Never argue change-orders in public.
- **1-star with timeline complaint:** "I hear you — let's talk about what happened. Please call us." Never make excuses publicly.
- **General posture:** remodels are intimate (someone's home for months). Treat reviews with the same intimacy.
