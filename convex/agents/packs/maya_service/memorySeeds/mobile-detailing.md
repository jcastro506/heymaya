# MEMORY.md seed — Mobile detailing

Domain priors for mobile auto-detailing businesses. Loaded as the curated MEMORY.md initial seed when `business.serviceTypes` includes `mobile-detailing`.

<!-- section:domain_priors:start -->
## Domain priors

- **Customer concerns rank in this order:** convenience (we come to you) > finish quality (paint correction, interior thoroughness) > water/power access handled > price.
- **Reviews almost always mention:** before/after photos, the specific service tier (interior detail, paint correction, ceramic coat), whether the detailer asked first about water/power, attention to detail (door jambs, vents, cup holders).
- **Photos are the entire moat.** Mobile detailing posts without before/after pairs are wasted posts. Reels of the polishing process outperform static finishes 3-5×.
- **Service tiers** (express wash → full detail → paint correction → ceramic coat) are sharply differentiated. Reviewers reference the tier explicitly. Operator's tier names go in MEMORY.md after first observation.
- **Add-on close rates** (pet hair, headlight restoration, engine bay) vary by season + customer demographics.
<!-- section:domain_priors:end -->

<!-- section:seasonal_calendar:start -->
## Seasonal calendar

- **Spring (March-May):** post-winter clay-bar + paint correction surge. Highest discretionary spend.
- **Pre-summer (May-June):** ceramic coat installs (UV protection seasonality).
- **Summer (June-August):** interior details (sand, sweat, kids); pollen/sap season.
- **Fall (September-November):** pre-winter prep — undercarriage, leather conditioning.
- **Winter (December-February):** slow for outdoor services in cold zones; interior + headlight + engine-bay work continues.
<!-- section:seasonal_calendar:end -->

<!-- section:brand_safety:start -->
## Brand-safety memory

- **Never quote tier pricing without operator approval.** Vehicle size + condition + add-ons — too many variables.
- **Never imply paint-correction outcomes without seeing the vehicle.** "Will this fix the swirls?" gets "I'd want to see it in person — happy to do a free assessment."
- **Ceramic coat warranty** language is brand-specific; never quote warranty terms operator hasn't confirmed.
- **Customer-vehicle photos** in public content require explicit permission; license plates blurred; never tie a vehicle to a named customer without permission.
- **Water/power requirements** ("can you come to my apartment?") are operator-owned scheduling Q's; don't assume.
<!-- section:brand_safety:end -->

## Review reply patterns (mobile-detailing-specific)

- **5-star with finish praise:** lead with the specific detail mentioned ("door jambs spotless"); reinforce attention-to-detail moat.
- **5-star with convenience praise:** thank, reinforce the mobile-comes-to-you positioning.
- **3-star with finish complaint:** "I'd want to come back and re-do anything that didn't meet your expectations — please call." Free re-do offer.
- **1-star with damage complaint** (paint scratched, interior stained): flag for operator-only — damage claims need operator handling.
