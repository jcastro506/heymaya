# HeyMaya — Maya for Creators

Maya is a creator's assistant for TikTok and Instagram. After onboarding she watches every post a creator has made and gets to know them; several times a day she watches the creators they admire and their lane and texts them ideas shaped to them, with links; she reads their calendar to plan filming around their life; she gives a grounded opinion on links and drafts; once a week she tells them what worked and why. She lives in Telegram. The web is a view.

This branch (`creator`) is a clean sheet. It shares no history with the previous product, which is preserved on the `legacy` branch and tag `legacy-2026-09-01` and keeps running until cutover.
